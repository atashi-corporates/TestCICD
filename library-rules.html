LIBRARY GUIDELINES

Library Rules

Guidelines for a comfortable and useful reading environment.

01

Maintain Silence

Keep conversations and device volume low in reading areas.

02

Carry Your College ID

Keep a valid college identity card available.

03

Return Books on Time

Return borrowed materials by the prescribed due date.

04

Handle Books Carefully

Avoid folding, tearing or marking pages.

05

No Food or Drinks

Keep food and drinks outside reading and book areas.

06

Follow Library Timings

Use the library during published working hours.
